## SLIDE 10 — CONTENT SAFETY

### Built for 35,000 feet

All AI-generated content follows strict inflight safety rules:

- Positive and welcoming tone only
- No violence, crime, or disasters in any content
- No anxiety-inducing topics (terrorism, crashes, severe weather warnings)

## SLIDE 12 — ARCHITECTURE (SIMPLE VIEW)

### Clean. Scalable. Offline-first.

```
  +---------------------------+
  |    PASSENGER DEVICE        |
  |    (Vueling App / Browser) |
  |    [Pre-cached content]    |
  +------------+---------------+
               |
               |  Silent push (content pre-loaded day before)
               |  WiFi (onboard fallback to edge cache)
               v
  +------------+---------------+
  |     API GATEWAY             |
  |     (FastAPI Backend)       |
  +--+-------+-------+-------+-+
     |       |       |       |
     v       v       v       v
  +-----+ +-----+ +-----+ +--------+
  |GEMINI| |WEATHER| |NEWS| |PAYMENT |
  |  AI  | | API  | | API| |GATEWAY |
  +-----+ +-----+ +-----+ +--------+
     |                         |
     +-- Content, Translation  +-- Food orders, real-time payments
```

**Content generation cadence:**
- Destination content: refreshed every **2 weeks** per destination
- Flight details: updated **daily** when crew/aircraft assigned
- News: refreshed **every 6 hours**
- Music playlists: refreshed **weekly**
- Weather: refreshed **every 12 hours**

**Offline strategy:**
- Silent push notifications pre-cache content on passenger devices day before flight
- Onboard WiFi serves from edge cache servers — minimal satellite bandwidth
- Only food orders and payments require real-time connectivity

## SLIDE 13 — BUSINESS IMPACT

### The numbers that matter

**Engagement play:**
- 34M passengers/year exposed to Vueling's own content ecosystem
- Every minute in the experience is a minute NOT spent on Instagram, Netflix, or a competitor's content

**Revenue play:**
- Pre-order food: if 8% of passengers pre-order at avg. ticket of 8-12 EUR = significant incremental revenue
- Onboard digital ordering adds another conversion layer
- Future: bookable restaurants, transfer reservations, attraction tickets — every content card becomes a commerce touchpoint

**Brand play:**
- First LCC with a true digital inflight experience
- Stronger than Ryanair, EasyJet, Wizz — none offer this
- Direct NPS driver: passengers who feel cared for come back and recommend

**Cost play:**
- AI content generation: ~0.005 EUR per passenger (negligible)
- Eliminates paper magazine: saves printing, logistics, weight (fuel savings)
- Cabin crew efficiency: pre-orders reduce aisle passes, faster turnarounds
